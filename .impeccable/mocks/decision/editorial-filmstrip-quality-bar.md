# Editorial Filmstrip quality bar

The approved composition is `.impeccable/mocks/decision/editorial-filmstrip.png`.

- **Frame:** a wide magazine spread with a small masthead, an oversized serif title on the left, a live research example on the right, and four colored chapter previews at the lower edge.
- **Depth:** cut paper, source slips, highlighted pages, and collage edges carry the visual weight. Research details remain readable over the art.
- **Lettering:** Playfair Display is the display voice; Lora is for prose and questions; Inter is for small structural text. The title should have the scale and confidence of an editorial cover.
- **Color:** warm white and ink support vivid violet, yellow, blue, coral, turquoise, and green, each tied to a research stage or action.
- **Ornament:** arrows, highlights, torn paper, and tactile print marks refer to the source-reading process. They should stay specific to research.
- **Motion:** the four stages are visible as a scrolling sequence. Normal and Deep tabs update the process. Motion should support reading and yield to reduced-motion preference.
- **Responsive:** the first mobile screen shows the product purpose, mode switch, question, and signup action. Each subsequent chapter is readable without a sticky blank track.
